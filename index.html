About

Booking, contracts, and venue relationships.

Rolam Management takes care of booking, contracts, and venue relationships so artists can stay focused on the work. The company is growing its roster carefully, with a SoCal base and a hands-on approach to each booking and introduction.

Founder Mitch Rolam brings a background in client management at American Express — the same attention to people and details he now brings to representing talent.

What I look for in DJs & artists

Not industry rules — just how I choose who I want to work with.

Craft & identity

A clear artistic point of view — something I can describe to a venue without guessing.

Clear goals

You know what rooms, cities, or nights you’re aiming for. That helps me pitch with intention.

Reliable communication

Quick, honest replies on availability, rates, and logistics. Bookings move when both sides stay clear.
